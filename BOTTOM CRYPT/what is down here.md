# what is down here

Nothing good. That is the point.

There is a bin in the corner of every page. Drag anything into it and it
comes down here. In the garden you can also just shove something off the
left or right edge of the screen and let it fall. Neither one waits for
you to have found this room first — throwing a thing away does not
require knowing where it lands.

Broken files. Books that will not open. Drafts that were bad on the day
and have not improved since. Anything a machine wrote that nobody asked
for.

Nothing is deleted. It is only down here, which is different. Full reset
brings the whole lot back up.
