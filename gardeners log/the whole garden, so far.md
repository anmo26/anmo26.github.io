# the whole garden, so far

everything this site is and does, on one page. the detail of how each piece
got here is in the folders beside this one — this is the map.

## what it is

a folder on my laptop, published as it is. every folder is a page, every
file is a thing you can open, and things sit where i put them in finder.
it updates when i change the folder.

## the rooms

- **THE GARDEN** — a tree you grow by clicking at its base
- **MOONLIGHT** — only open after dark. the moon at tonight's real phase,
  and a rocket you fuel a click at a time
- **THE OCEAN**, **BOTTOM CRYPT**, **SUN LEVEL** — each a world of its own
- **THE BAR** — a record playing, badly and differently every time
- **THE FORUM** — a real message board, separate from the notes
- **THE FIELD** — a rice field where everybody in the room hears the same
  second of the same record at once
- **LIBRARY!!!!!** — the shelf i actually read from

most of the rooms are secret until somebody finds the way in.

## the sky

on the **universe** paper only, and only there. a real planetarium for
wherever you are: the sun's actual path (high in summer, low in winter), the
moon at its real phase, the five bright planets, 210 real named stars and 28
constellations that write their own names. weather is real too — rain,
snow, cloud and fog arrive on the page when they arrive outside.

on the front page there are dials: turn to face any direction, tip your
head up or down, wind time two months either way, or press **drift** to
watch a day pass in about a minute and a half.

shooting stars, about one every half minute. you will usually miss them.

## the taskbar

- **sound** — the room's sound, and the music player
- **notes** — show the wall of notes, or leave one (an idea, or a chat)
- **mini fig** — a little guy who pogos, runs or flies after your cursor
- **the page** — the plants, the clock, other people's cursors, and which
  paper the site is printed on (bone, universe, pocari, olive, ink)
- **tidy up** — slide everything back where it belongs
- **full reset** — start over

a new visitor arrives quietly: no notes, no music, the player tucked away,
the mini fig resting. everything is there to find.

## the notes

anybody can stick a note on any page. **ideas** are about the site and get
read as suggestions; **chat** is everything else. notes can be replied to.
only whoever wrote a note can take it down.

an idea becomes a change only when i say so. once it is built, it comes
off the wall, and what happened to it is kept in `suggestions/`.

## where everything else is

- `site updates/` — every update, by day and time, with what changed
- `suggestions/the wall so far.md` — every note ever left, and its outcome
- `plans/` — ideas for later, and how a note turns into a change
- the card on the front page — the newest update, what i planned, what is
  suggested, and what is still undecided
